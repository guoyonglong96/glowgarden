Glowgarden is a lantern-defense game: build Ember, Frost and Bloom lanterns on the garden plots to hold the Heart Lantern through 10 nights of shadow moths. Earn gold for every moth stopped, upgrade your lanterns, and keep all 22 hearts of light alive. Lanterns of different kinds standing close together harmonize for +15% damage, so where you plant each kind matters as much as which kind you plant. Kills in quick succession build a chain that pays bonus gold, and the garden itself — pond, glowing grove, stone lantern, sweeping beacon light — is all part of the night.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Click a lantern card, then a glowing plot to build; SPACE starts a night. Keys 1-3 pick a lantern, P pauses, F toggles speed, M mutes, R restarts after a win or loss.
